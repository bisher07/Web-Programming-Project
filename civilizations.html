Civilizations

Six civilizations side by side

Where they lived, how long they lasted, how they wrote, and what they left behind.

c. 3500 – 539 BC

Mesopotamia

The land between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers, where the first cities, the wheel and writing appeared.

c. 3100 – 30 BC

Egypt

A kingdom built along the Nile, ruled by pharaohs for three thousand years and famous for its pyramids.

c. 2000 BC – AD 1500

Maya

City-states in the rainforests of Central America, masters of astronomy and the calendar.

c. 1600 BC – AD 220

Ancient China

Dynasties along the Yellow River that gave the world paper, silk and the Great Wall.

c. 800 – 146 BC

Greece

Independent city-states around the Aegean Sea, the birthplace of democracy, philosophy and theatre.

753 BC – AD 476

Rome

One city on the Tiber that grew into an empire around the whole Mediterranean Sea.
